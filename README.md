\# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI



\## 📊 Project Overview



The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It helps analyze employee information and provides insights into workforce demographics, employee distribution, promotion eligibility, retirement trends, job satisfaction, and overtime.



The main objective is to help HR teams understand workforce data and make data-driven decisions.



\## 🛠️ Tools \& Technologies



\- Microsoft Power BI

\- Power Query

\- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

\- Data Visualization



\## 📌 Dashboard Pages



\### 1. Home Dashboard



Provides an overview of employee information through interactive KPIs and visualizations.



\*\*Key Metrics:\*\*

\- Total Employees: 1,470

\- Male Employees: 882 (60%)

\- Female Employees: 588 (40%)

\- Employees Due for Promotion: 260

\- Employees Due for Retirement: 29

\- Active Employees: 98.03%



\*\*Visualizations:\*\*

\- Gender distribution

\- Employee count by job level

\- Service years analysis

\- Distance from office

\- Promotion and retirement eligibility



!\[Home Dashboard](Screenshots/01\_Home.png)



\### 2. Action Dashboard



Focuses on identifying employees who require HR attention.



\*\*Key Features:\*\*

\- Employees due for promotion

\- Employees due for retirement

\- Employee-level information for HR action planning



!\[Action Dashboard](Screenshots/02\_Action.png)



\### 3. Detail Dashboard



Provides detailed insights into employee satisfaction, job roles, and workforce trends.



\*\*Key Features:\*\*

\- Employee satisfaction by job level

\- Employee count by job role

\- Overtime analysis

\- Promotion and retirement analysis

\- Employee satisfaction indicators



!\[Detail Dashboard](Screenshots/03\_Detail.png)



\## 🔍 Key Insights



\- The organization has a total workforce of 1,470 employees.

\- Male employees represent 60% of the workforce, while female employees represent 40%.

\- 260 employees are identified as due for promotion.

\- 29 employees are identified as due for retirement.

\- The dashboard provides insights into employee satisfaction and overtime distribution.

\- HR teams can identify employees requiring promotion and retirement-related attention.
